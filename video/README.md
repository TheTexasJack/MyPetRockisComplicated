# Video and Animation
Plan short-form motion comics, title cards, animatics and eventually animated episodes. **Reuse canonical art and comics** rather than rebuilding every shot.

## Episode convention
`short-001/` may contain script, storyboard, shot list, animatic notes, credits and an export manifest. Binary videos belong under episode-specific `exports/` or an approved release storage location.

Keep animation achievable: limited motion, expressive posing, effective audio, and editorial timing.
