# Cross-Media Production Roadmap | v0.1

**Aim:** Establish one original universe and a cast that works first in short comic strips, then naturally adapts into animated shorts, motion comics, audio sketches and other media.

**Guiding constraint:** No unnecessary production escalation. Let each small published piece prove the tone and pipeline.

## Stage 1 · Canon and art foundation (current)
- Maintain Character and World Bibles; creator approves final choices.
- Finalize master visual references for the leads, R.O.C.K., Moxie and OptIn.
- Roster established: **12 recurring characters and 6 occasional NPC templates** in [CAST_ROSTER.md](../bible/CAST_ROSTER.md); establish any undeveloped designs and antagonists only when needed.
- Agree on signature expressions, silhouettes and color guides.
- Record the intended look and status of every character image.

**Deliverable:** Reliable, reusable character masters and a tiny test strip.

## Stage 2 · Comics (primary)
- Plan a three-to-six-panel debut strip using a single joke and clear character voices.
- Create thumbnail storyboard, script, panel art, lettered final.
- Build a reusable credits/intro/title-card template.
- Accumulate short episodic strips before considering an extended issue.
- Maintain comic continuity in the same shared canon.

**Deliverable:** Issue/strip 001 and a repeatable production process.

## Stage 3 · Motion comics and video
- Reuse approved comic visuals and layered assets where possible.
- Develop storyboard, animatic and short voice scripts.
- Choose simple limited-motion techniques: cuts, camera moves, mouth/eye changes, pans.
- Record/edit voices, sound effects and music with documented rights.
- Produce a short opening title/teaser before a full episode.

**Deliverable:** One short pilot clip, not a promise of a full animated season.

## Stage 4 · Additional media (optional)
- Audio sketches, short-form social posts, community-board prints and posters.
- Character cards, simple interactives or a game if the comic earns it.
- Merchandise concepts only after art/canon and usage rights are stable.

## Episode production checklist
Idea -> canon check -> script -> thumbnails -> art/asset prep -> edit/letter or animate -> continuity review -> creator approval -> final export -> archive.

## Open decisions
Setting, headquarters, undeveloped character designs, further antagonist roster, release cadence and audience rating remain open. Nothing here establishes a deadline.
