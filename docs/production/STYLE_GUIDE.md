# Visual Style Guide | v0.1

## Target
Bright, expressive **retro Saturday-morning adventure cartoon**, combining the visual wit of mid-century limited animation with the energy of 1980s science-fiction action shows. It should be clearly **original art**, not copies of existing franchise character designs.

## Foundational style
- Strong readable silhouettes; expressive poses and facial acting.
- Thick, confident ink outlines and compact shadow shapes.
- Flat, graphic cel colors, restrained shading, some vintage print texture.
- Dynamic wide title-card compositions and simple readable staging.
- Vintage sci-fi props: bold consoles, starfields, rock formations, oversized gadgets, playful orbital geometry.
- Comedy through posture and expression as much as dialogue.
- Keep imagery recognizable at poster, comic-panel and phone sizes.

## Character visual continuity
- **Upshot:** tired precog; dark hair with a light streak, worn expression, white/red sci-fi outfit. Sleep and psychic strain are visual motifs.
- **Optout:** expressive anthropomorphic goat hacker; goggles, utility gear, screen/tablet, **horizontal rectangular goat pupils**, consistently, even in small panels. Creator's manually corrected eye reference is authoritative.
- **Lash:** broad orange-armored engineered soldier, confident joyous expression, physically dynamic.
- **Despond:** blue-haired or blue-themed downcast cosmopolitan, stylized beret/scarf or cape, elegant bored body language and drink as a recurring prop.
- **R.O.C.K.:** **tiny hovering rock-shaped drone**, friendly eyes, subtle mechanical hardware/anti-grav. Avoid an oversized military robot look. The creator particularly favors the simple, silly rock-like iteration.
- **Moxie:** detailed appearance undecided; unpredictable, bright, and capable.
- **Sergeant Bellows:** exactly four feet tall, exaggerated short barrel-chested 1980s-cartoon sergeant, huge cap and moustache, commanding posture and furious expression. Show his dramatic height contrast with Lash **only in the composition**. Nobody ever comments on, reacts to, or adjusts posture for the height difference, not even subtly.
- **Bobby:** middle-aged anthropomorphic goat in a worn white undershirt, no necktie, no outer shirt; horizontal goat pupils. Tired ordinary dad energy.
- **OptIn:** vivid red hair, polished smile, purple corporate sci-fi uniform, immaculate presentation; cheerful and terrifyingly persuasive.

## Image hierarchy
1. **Creator-approved / edited** reference art wins.
2. First-pass reference art documents composition only, not immutable character details.
3. Generated variants are exploratory until manually reviewed.
4. No new appearance, costume or color tweak silently becomes canon.

## Production tips
- Character source art: high-resolution PNG or lossless working file; record references, edits, and status.
- Separate line art, color layers and background when possible.
- Prefer dialogue-free character masters, then add text in layout software.
- Comic layouts and video storyboards can reuse character silhouettes and backgrounds.
- Export final publishing derivatives separately from working assets.

## Known generation issue
AI renders sometimes misdraw Optout's goat eyes as human vertical pupils, spectacles, or two horizontal slashes. **Correct them in post; do not approve the erroneous eyes as a design change.**
