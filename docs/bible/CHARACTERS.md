# My Pet Rock Is Complicated
## Character Bible | Working Draft v0.1

**Genre:** Original retro science-fiction adventure comedy, with the loud sincerity, broad silhouettes, and dramatic cliffhangers of an early-1980s Saturday-morning cartoon.

**Premise:** Four spectacularly ill-matched adventurers and one inexplicably cheerful little drone are repeatedly trusted with problems they are only marginally qualified to solve. The threats are real. The heroes are capable. Their teamwork is the dangerous part.

**Canon labels:** `Established` means decided in conversation. `Proposed` means a working writing choice awaiting approval. This story is original and does not require Lancer or SSC settings, brands, or lore.

## Main cast

### UPSHOT | The Exhausted Precog
**Status:** Name and premise established; dialogue examples proposed.

**Role:** The visionary who can see what's coming but struggles to explain it before the others do something else. Genetically engineered precognition is his great gift and his never-ending headache.

**Attitude:** Smart, dry, sleep-deprived, frequently halfway through a thought. He isn't cowardly or indifferent; he's simply overtaxed. Under the fatigue he genuinely wants to help.

**Typically does:** Starts a brilliant warning, forgets the middle, sees three catastrophes simultaneously, reaches for coffee, winces when Moxie invents an entirely new option.

**Might say:**
- "I've seen how this ends. Give me a minute to remember which ending."
- "Whatever you do, don't... never mind."
- "I need a nap from tomorrow."

**Comic engine:** He can predict nearly everything except Moxie's choices, which are too random to model. Their connection is mentor-and-apprentice / reluctant coworkers, **not** romantic.

### OPTOUT | The Compassionate Hacker
**Status:** Name, goat identity, personality direction, and horizontal goat pupils established.

**Role:** The engineer and systems hacker with a good heart and no instinct to respect a locked door.

**Attitude:** Friendly, curious, casually subversive. To him, barriers are invitations to improvise. He genuinely cares about the people he's helping, even when his methods create three more problems.

**Typically does:** Opens forbidden control panels, improves hostile machines without asking, rewrites a security system while everyone is arguing about procedure, tries to rescue the machine as well as its operator.

**Might say:**
- "It said access denied, not access impossible."
- "I fixed it! ...What was it supposed to do?"
- "Rules are just suggestions with better typography."

**Visual canon:** Anthropomorphic goat. **Horizontal rectangular goat pupils** in every illustration, even when angry or surprised. The creator may correct the pupils manually in post-production.

**Comic engine:** Arch-nemesis to OptIn, who can turn any interaction into a binding agreement.

### LASH | The Cheerfully Engineered Super-Soldier
**Status:** Name, super-soldier role and broad attitude established; exact powers and the Le Petomane joke remain open.

**Role:** The muscle. Bred for one purpose, enthusiastically certain that every situation needs decisive action.

**Attitude:** Booming, proud, fearless, and generally delighted to be here. Not cruel. He has a surprisingly innocent faith in straightforward solutions.

**Typically does:** Volunteers to tackle objects that aren't enemies, lifts something that should have been unlocked first, celebrates before the plan is finished, accidentally turns a minor obstacle into an action sequence.

**Might say:**
- "Good news! I found the problem!"
- "Was that door important?"
- "Don't worry. I've got a plan!" *(This worries everybody.)*

**Comic engine:** His confidence arrives several seconds before anyone understands the situation. The name's Mel Brooks / Le Petomane allusion is a possible recurring wink, not yet a fixed ability.

### DESPOND | The Disappointed Cosmopolitan
**Status:** Name, mood, and cultivated appearance established; dialogue examples proposed.

**Role:** The critic, skeptic, and cultural snob. He could be genuinely useful if the universe would stop being so embarrassing.

**Attitude:** Wry, melancholic, fastidious, opinionated. He dislikes chaos but is often correct about its consequences. His finest qualities should emerge when others need him, rather than making him a one-note complainer.

**Typically does:** Surveys a disaster with a cocktail in hand, critiques a civilization's taste, offers reluctant expertise, somehow remains impeccably dressed through an explosion.

**Might say:**
- "I expected very little. Somehow I'm still disappointed."
- "This is why civilized people make reservations."
- "Must we save the galaxy *before* lunch?"

**Comic engine:** The team's running commentary on itself. Let him occasionally surprise everyone with an act of real generosity.

### R.O.C.K. | Robotic Organism Companion, K-Class
**Status:** Name, acronym, and tiny hovering rock-drone concept established.

**Role:** The team's pet, companion, accidental conscience, and most reliable witness.

**Attitude:** Cheerful, loyal, untroubled. Its expressive eyes and noises convey more emotional intelligence than some of the heroes manage in dialogue.

**Typically does:** Floats after the team, observes terrible plans, projects simple icons, offers a flashlight at inconvenient moments, celebrates small victories, gently bumps an inattentive hero.

**Might say/display:**
- "BEEP!" 🙂
- `CAUTION: PLAN NOT FOUND`
- `FRIENDS DETECTED: 4` *(Whether they deserve it is another question.)*

**Visual canon:** Small rock-shaped hovering drone, not a full-sized robot or an ordinary stationary pebble. Keep it approachable and absurdly happy.

## Recurring support

### MOXIE | The Unpredictable Apprentice
**Status:** Name, premise, and non-romantic relationship with Upshot established.

**Role:** Upshot's enthusiastic apprentice, whose choices are too random for his precognition to predict. There is no cosmic explanation necessary. She is simply Moxie.

**Attitude:** Bright, impulsive, inventive, capable. Her ideas sound ridiculous until they work. She likes women; **she is not a love interest for Upshot**.

**Typically does:** Misunderstands a warning in a creative way, tries an unexpectedly effective solution, asks an innocent question that unravels Upshot's certainty.

**Might say:**
- "What if we give the space squid a birthday cake?"
- "You can see the future. Why are you always surprised?"

**Established exchange:**

**UPSHOT:** "Are you flirting with me?"

**MOXIE** *(deadpan, a little disappointed)*: "I like women."

**UPSHOT:** "I... didn't see that coming."

**MOXIE:** "You never do!"

**Additional recurring-support slots:** Three remain **uncreated**. Do not populate them by accident just to fill a roster.

## Recurring antagonist

### OPTIN | The Smile in the Fine Print
**Status:** Name, villain premise, and signature appearance established.

**Role:** Optout's archnemesis. A radiant corporate manipulator who uses hidden terms, dark patterns, and presumed consent to claim control over other people's lives.

**Attitude:** Immaculate, ingratiating, unshakably polite. She is most frightening when she's nicest. She never thinks she is cheating; she thinks everybody agreed.

**Typically does:** Auto-checks consent boxes, buries the dangerous clause in cheerful fine print, reframes coercion as a customer benefit, thanks victims for cooperating, produces an agreement no one remembers accepting.

**Might say:**
- "By opening this door, you've already agreed!"
- "Your objection has been recorded as enthusiastic participation."
- "You may opt out at any time. Processing takes fourteen years."

**Visual canon:** Retro-corporate sci-fi woman, red hair, purple formal uniform, polished smile. Her primary established illustration is a single-character image, without labels or other figures.

## Relationships and comedy rules

- **Upshot + Moxie:** A man who knows tomorrow versus a woman who makes tomorrow improvise. Strictly non-romantic.
- **Optout + OptIn:** One circumvents systems; the other makes the systems impossible to refuse.
- **Lash + Despond:** Enthusiastic action versus reluctant commentary.
- **R.O.C.K. + everyone:** The friendly little constant that can turn a disastrous group into something resembling a family.
- **All four leads:** Competent in their specialties, spectacularly unreliable as a committee. Avoid making them merely stupid or cruel.

## Tone and production rules

1. **Saturday-cartoon readability:** Distinct silhouettes, fast banter, obvious stakes, oversized expressions, silly cliffhangers.
2. **Earned heart:** Real compassion beneath the jokes. The characters eventually matter beyond the punchlines.
3. **Original world:** Keep comic continuity independent of Lancer, its proprietary terms and organizations, and the real-life players who helped spark the idea.
4. **No accidental personal attacks:** Fictionalize and evolve the characters; their comedy should stand without knowing anybody at the gaming table.
5. **Art continuity:** Use approved portraits as visual references. Manual corrections made by the creator take precedence over generated inconsistencies.
6. **Open questions stay open:** Three companion characters, the main villain roster, world name, powers and backstories need later decisions.
