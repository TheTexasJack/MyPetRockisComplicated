## Model-production conventions

- Use stable, lowercase `asset key` values for filenames and references. Names above remain the canonical display names.
- Production assets are not automatically canon: a generated portrait or pose requires creator review.
- Character consistency (anatomy, finger counts, proportions, attachment positions, costume and prop continuity) must be established in approved model sheets **before** high-volume panel assembly.
- Named characters are distinct identities; NPC templates are interchangeable production roles.
- No additional named NPCs or supporting cast members are implied by this roster.
- The creator may expand or change the roster when the story warrants it.
