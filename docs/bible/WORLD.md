# World Bible | Draft v0.1

## Established series premise
*My Pet Rock Is Complicated* is an **original** science-fiction adventure comedy with a bright retro Saturday-morning cartoon sensibility. Four capable-but-ill-matched adventurers and their little rock-like companion drone are trusted with galactic emergencies. The danger is usually real; the group's approach to teamwork is often what makes it worse.

The cast is **Upshot, Optout, Lash, Despond, and R.O.C.K.** Recurring character **Moxie** is Upshot's enthusiastically random apprentice; antagonist **OptIn** uses deceptive consent machinery to frustrate Optout. See [Character Bible](CHARACTERS.md).

## Story engine
1. Someone brings the heroes an apparently manageable problem.
2. Each lead suggests a solution shaped by their own specialty and personality.
3. Unintended consequences escalate in a silly but intelligible way.
4. R.O.C.K. witnesses or helps expose the obvious solution.
5. Resolution leaves room for warmth, character growth, and another impossible assignment.

The team can be ridiculous without being incompetent at everything. There is room for heroism under the jokes.

## Format-independent continuity
Comics establish a shared canon; animated shorts, audio sketches, posters, promotional mini-comics and future games may reuse it. An adaptation may cut scenes for runtime but shouldn't silently change canonical relationships, characters or historical events.

## Established boundaries
- **Independent IP:** no direct use of Lancer, Harrison Armory, SSC, NHP or other setting-specific franchises in the publishable universe.
- Avoid making real players identifiable targets of the satire. The comic characters gain separate biographies and relationships.
- Moxie and Upshot are **not** a romance. Her randomness defeats his precognition without requiring complicated temporal lore.
- R.O.C.K. expands to **Robotic Organism Companion, K-Class**.
- OptIn's villainy is cheerful coercion through consent traps, dark patterns and fine print.

## Deliberately undecided

- Team origin, headquarters and how its members first met.
- Precognition's limitations beyond Moxie's unpredictability.
- Lash's exact engineered abilities; whether the Le Petomane allusion becomes a joke.
- Whether R.O.C.K. speaks in sentences or mostly beeps.
- Three additional recurring support cast members, and main-villain lineup.
- First season arc and core origin story.

Never turn these open questions into facts without creator approval.
