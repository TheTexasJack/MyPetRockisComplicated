# Canon & Decision Log

**Creator's final decision governs all changes.** GitHub is the working master for this project.

## Labels
- **Established**: explicitly decided in collaboration.
- **Proposed**: creative suggestion for review, not yet canon.
- **Approved art**: creator-corrected or creator-selected reference that overrides raw generated variants.
- **Open**: intentionally unmade decision.

## Canon-first workflow
1. Capture candidate ideas under Proposed/Open.
2. Review and explicitly approve names, relationships, designs and story facts.
3. Update the relevant Bible entry and record the decision below.
4. Avoid silent continuity changes when adapting to comic, video, or audio.
