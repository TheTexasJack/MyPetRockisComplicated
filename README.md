# My Pet Rock Is Complicated 🪨
An original retro science-fiction comedy series about four astonishingly mismatched adventurers and their loyal little rock-shaped drone, R.O.C.K. They are the galaxy's last hope. Unfortunately.

**Formats:** comics first; animated shorts, video, audio, and other media later. All productions share one continuity and character bible.

## Canon rules
1. **Creator approval wins.** Work-in-progress documents are proposals, not automatically canon.
2. **The four heroes are capable specialists; group coordination is their weakness.** Let the stories be funny *and* have heart.
3. **Original universe.** Do not import proprietary Lancer factions, setting terms, or other copyrighted franchise material into the published comic.
4. **Corrections beat raw generations.** Edited/approved art takes precedence over AI draft renders, especially Optout's distinctive horizontal goat pupils.
5. **Reuse before rebuilding.** One shared design/bible supports print, motion, audio, and promotional art.

## Current status
**Preproduction, v0.1.** Character names and personalities are established; three additional support characters, primary villains, the setting, and the first comic are still open. Nothing is announced as published.
