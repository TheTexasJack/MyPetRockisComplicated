# Comics
Short comic strips are the first release format for *My Pet Rock Is Complicated*.

## Per-issue convention
`issue-001/` may contain:
- `outline.md`: setup, escalation, punchline, emotional beat.
- `script.md`: numbered panels, visuals, dialogue and captions.
- `storyboard.md`: rough staging and source-asset references.
- `exports/`: approved finished pages, social cuts or print-ready files.

Keep the core joke understandable without a lore encyclopedia. The recurring cast's distinct voices should drive the comedy.
